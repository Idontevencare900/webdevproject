Introduction

Whenever you log into a website, add something to your shopping cart, or view an advertisement targeted at your preferences, chances are that a tiny piece of information known as a cookie is being used. Cookies enable websites to store information about you for future use. Cookies make our online experience more convenient, but they also have certain issues when it comes to privacy. The purpose of this website is to explain browser cookies and how they work.

Key Terms

  • Cookie: A Small piece of data sent from a website and stored on a user's web browser.
  • Session cookie: Temporary data files stored in a browser's memory (RAM) that track a user's activity during a single visit to a website.
  • Third-party cookie: A cookie that is created and set by a domain different from the specific website you are currently visiting
  • First-party cookie: A cookie that is created and stored directly by the specific website you are visiting
  • Persistent cookie: Cookies that are stored on a user's hard drive or device that remain active even after the web browser is closed
  • Tracking: The collection, storage, and analysis of a user's online activities and interactions across websites.
